Account & Data Deletion

Under Google Play requirements, users can request complete deletion of their account and all associated data.

How to Request Deletion

Send an email to: ajunamike@gmail.com

Subject: MediSync Account and Data Deletion Request

Include: The email address associated with your app profile.

Deletion Timeline

All user authentication records and synced routine data will be permanently wiped within 30 days of request receipt.
