Account & Data Deletion

Under Google Play's User Data policy, users can request complete deletion of their account profile and all associated data.

How to Submit a Deletion Request

Support Email: medisyncmedicalsolutions@gmail.com

Subject Line: MediSync Account and Data Deletion Request

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Send an email to medisyncmedicalsolutions@gmail.com from your registered email account.
  2. Set the subject to MediSync Account and Data Deletion Request.
  3. Provide your app account identifier or associated email address in the body of the message.

What Data Is Removed

  • User profile records and account credentials.
  • All synced habit records, routines, and historical logs.

Retention Timeline

All associated account data and server records are permanently deleted within 30 days of receiving your verified request.
