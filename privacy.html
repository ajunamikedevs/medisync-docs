Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026

MediSync: Habit Tracker ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how our mobile application handles user data.

Offline-First Architecture: All habit records, daily routines, task logs, and completion histories are stored directly on your local device. The application does not require a remote server to function.

1. Permissions

  • Notifications & Exact Alarms: Used strictly to deliver user-scheduled routine reminders and habit prompts at the specific times you designate.

2. Data Collection & Sharing

We do not collect, rent, sell, or trade your personal habit or routine information to third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks.

3. Data Deletion

Because habit data is stored locally, uninstalling the app or clearing application cache removes your data instantly. If you have an account or synced data, you can request permanent deletion at our Account & Data Deletion Page.
